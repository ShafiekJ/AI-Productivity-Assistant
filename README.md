# Monocle

Monocle is a sleek monochrome AI workspace for getting work out the door: draft emails, summarize
meetings, plan your day and week, digest research links, and chat with a warm workplace assistant.

## Features

### Overview dashboard
- One entry point to every tool, with short descriptions of what each does.
- Responsible AI disclaimer covering review expectations, data handling and accountability.

### Email generator
- Describe the occasion and generate a polished, professional email.
- Pick tone and length to match the audience.
- Save drafts locally and reload or delete them from a saved-drafts gallery.

### Meeting notes summarizer
- Paste raw notes and get a structured summary.
- Extracts key points, decisions, action items and deadlines.
- Voice input: dictate notes and have them transcribed straight into the editor.

### Task planner
- Generate daily and weekly schedules with one click, then fill the day automatically.
- Priority list per day with drag-and-drop reordering.
- Mark tasks done to strike matching schedule blocks out.

### Research assistant
- Paste an article link and get a concise summary of it.
- Automatically suggests related reading on similar topics — no extra prompting needed.

### Assistant chat (Mono)
- Threaded conversations with streaming replies, saved between visits.
- Warm, concise, human tone — with the occasional work-appropriate joke.
- Starter prompts for drafting, prioritising and sanity-checking work.

### Across the app
- Dark monochrome design system with a soft crimson gradient wash.
- Responsive layout with a sidebar that closes instantly when navigating on mobile.
- Local-first storage for chats, drafts and tasks.
